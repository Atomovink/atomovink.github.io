AI for Materials Discovery

Making AI-discovered materials worth trusting.

Atomovink is an independent research lab working where machine learning meets materials science. We build open, reproducible research and tools that help scientists tell which AI predictions to act on, and which to question.

What we work on

Three areas, one question: can we trust it?

Validation of AI-generated materials

Stability, novelty and reliability checks for crystals proposed by generative models, using machine-learning interatomic potentials and open databases.

Experiment design with small data

Uncertainty-aware models and Bayesian optimisation that suggest the next experiment for formulation and materials R&D teams.

Scientific data from literature

Turning papers into structured, checkable materials data: compositions, synthesis conditions and measured properties.

Research

Current experiment

EXP-001 · In progress

Why does AI-discovered stability collapse on more complete reference hulls?

When the reference database grows, the share of generative-model crystals that count as stable, unique and novel drops sharply, but nobody has explained why. EXP-001 separates lost novelty from displaced stability, finds which chemistries are fragile, and tests whether fragility can be predicted.

About

An independent lab, starting small

Atomovink is founded by Navin, a materials science engineer with a background in software and AI.

The lab is early-stage and self-funded. We publish everything we can: code, data manifests, methods and limitations, so that others can check and build on the work. ML predictions are reported as predictions, never as experimental facts.
